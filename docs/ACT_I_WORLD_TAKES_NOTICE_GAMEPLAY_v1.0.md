# Argentina Rise - Act I: World Takes Notice - Gameplay v1.0

## Scope

This document designs the first act of the Global Crisis as gameplay and narrative design only.

It begins when Mexico has been integrated into Argentina. It ends when the player has completed the first international crisis and has earned the right to choose a geopolitical orientation. It contains no code, IDs, localisation, technical implementation, or new gameplay content definitions.

The act's central question is:

> Can Argentina turn a continental victory into a credible international position before the world turns that victory into a threat?

## 1. Act identity

### Narrative objective

Make the Mexican integration feel like a historic threshold rather than the end of a conquest chain. The world must recognise that Argentina has acquired a new scale, while Argentina must recognise that scale is not yet legitimacy.

### Gameplay objective

Teach the player the Global Crisis loop without overwhelming them:

1. receive a world reaction;
2. consolidate the conditions that make Argentina credible;
3. choose a practical agenda;
4. demonstrate responsibility, capacity or restraint;
5. receive a more informed foreign response;
6. arrive at the orientation threshold with several viable futures.

### Player fantasy

The player begins as the victor of a continental campaign and ends as a stateperson who understands that the world has started to calculate around Argentina.

The power fantasy is deliberately unfinished. Argentina is not yet the author of a world order. It is now impossible to ignore, and the player must decide what that attention becomes.

## 2. Act structure at a glance

| Phase | Function | Player activity | External activity | Emotional movement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I. Integration becomes recognition | Establish the new reality | Stabilise and observe | The world notices | Pride becomes exposure |
| II. The proof window | Give the player room to play | Consolidate continental capacity | Markets and neighbours assess | Exposure becomes responsibility |
| III. The first courtship | Turn attention into choices | Prepare an agenda and protect autonomy | Major powers signal interest or caution | Responsibility becomes agency |
| IV. The demonstration | Ask Argentina to prove its claims | Deliver one visible, limited commitment | Partners test reliability | Agency becomes credibility |
| V. The orientation threshold | Close the opening crisis | Compare viable futures | The world revises its expectations | Credibility becomes direction |

The act must contain alternating beats:

**major narrative moment -> operational play window -> player choice point -> construction and management -> new narrative moment -> renewed play window.**

No two major narrative moments should arrive without the player having meaningful work to perform in between.

## 3. Phase I: Integration becomes recognition

### Purpose

Mexico has become a political fact. The first phase does not ask the player to celebrate it again. It asks the player to understand that a new Argentine frontier has changed the strategic map.

### Narrative objective

Move the story from victory to consequence. Buenos Aires sees a new continental map; the international press, major capitals and the UN frame it as an issue that exceeds Argentina and Mexico.

### Gameplay objective

Introduce the player to the idea that external attention is a resource and a risk. The player must see that future international options depend on what Argentina does with its newly integrated scale.

### What the player is concretely doing

- evaluates the immediate state of the continental economy;
- checks supply, production, energy and transport capacity across the enlarged country;
- identifies which sectors are now strategically visible to foreign powers;
- protects the basic functioning of the integrated territory before making global promises;
- absorbs the end of a war and prepares for a period in which perception matters.

### What major powers are doing

- the United States reassesses hemispheric security and begins to decide whether Argentina requires containment, prudence or dialogue;
- Russia evaluates whether the Argentine rise creates an independent counterweight or an unstable risk;
- China evaluates whether continental scale can become a dependable economic partner;
- European powers weigh legal unease against commercial opportunity;
- neighbouring states observe whether Argentine integration is followed by responsibility or further appetite.

### Player emotion

Pride mixed with exposure. The player has succeeded, but the game makes clear that success has made Argentina visible in rooms it did not previously enter.

### New opportunities

- first contact with a wider diplomatic field;
- new economic relevance through continental scale;
- ability to frame Argentina as responsible, autonomous or multipolar;
- stronger regional demand for Argentine leadership.

### New responsibilities

- govern the integrated territory credibly;
- avoid making the world read Argentina as permanently expansionist;
- protect the foundations of trade, energy and security before pursuing prestige;
- recognise that Latin America will judge Argentine conduct before global powers do.

### Perception change

**World:** Argentina is no longer a regional state with ambitions. It is a continental power whose future orientation matters.

**Inside Argentina:** citizens and institutions begin to understand that victory has created obligations as well as pride.

**New visible objective for the player:** make Argentina governable at continental scale before attempting to make it influential at global scale.

### Rhythm

The recognition sequence should be concentrated and memorable, then release the player into a genuine play window. It must not be followed immediately by a flood of foreign demands.

### Existing Millennium Dawn foundations

- international recognition and news patterns provide the language for world acknowledgement;
- relationship systems support the distinction between alarm, caution and opening;
- market systems support the idea that expanded scale produces commercial attention;
- the existing Argentina Rise Mexico and conference structures prove that a political threshold can lead into player-led work rather than an automatic reward.

## 4. Phase II: The proof window

### Purpose

This is the first major operational interval. It is the answer to the question: what does the player do after the world notices, before it answers in detail?

### Narrative objective

Show that the world is watching whether Argentina can govern the consequences of its own rise. The story is no longer about taking Mexico; it is about proving that Argentina is not improvising its future.

### Gameplay objective

Make domestic capacity, regional stewardship and economic sovereignty feel like geopolitical preparation.

### What the player is concretely doing

- stabilises continental logistics, energy, production and public capacity;
- determines which export sectors, industrial chains and transport routes can support international commitments;
- decides how much economic capacity remains domestic and how much can be used for regional or external relationships;
- prepares a security posture that protects the country without broadcasting panic or aggression;
- tends to the immediate trust needs of Latin American partners and neighbours;
- reviews exposure to debt, market pressure, supply disruption and external dependence.

### What major powers are doing

- the United States monitors whether Argentina is consolidating or preparing further expansion;
- Russia and China assess whether Argentina's scale is durable enough for serious dialogue;
- European commercial actors observe stability, law and reliability;
- markets price governance quality rather than only military success;
- Latin American states decide whether Argentine leadership appears useful, threatening or conditional.

### Player emotion

Responsibility. The player should feel that ordinary work has acquired strategic meaning. A transport corridor, a stable energy system or a reliable export capacity now affects how other powers treat Argentina.

### New opportunities

- demonstrate that Argentina can deliver on an international promise;
- create the material base for future trade, infrastructure and security relationships;
- establish regional legitimacy before seeking global leadership;
- prepare multiple diplomatic paths rather than committing early.

### New responsibilities

- avoid overextending the continental economy;
- ensure that new commitments do not undermine domestic confidence;
- demonstrate restraint to neighbours without appearing weak;
- create capacity before asking others to trust Argentina with a larger role.

### Perception change

**World:** Argentina may be more than a disruptive victor; it may be a durable actor.

**Inside Argentina:** the public begins to see continental power as a daily administrative and economic task, not only a military achievement.

**New visible objective for the player:** build the credibility needed to choose an international agenda rather than accepting one chosen by others.

### Rhythm

This phase needs the longest uninterrupted period of play in the act. The player should have room to make several connected economic, regional and security choices before another major narrative interruption.

### Existing Millennium Dawn foundations

- market access and contract systems can express trade reliability and exposure;
- investment and debt systems can express the cost of growth and foreign confidence;
- intelligence systems can express awareness, counter-pressure and strategic visibility;
- the existing Argentine energy, resources and procurement structures demonstrate that capacity can already be made playable inside the submod.

## 5. Phase III: The first courtship

### Purpose

Attention becomes a choice. The act must make clear that great powers do not merely react to Argentina; they attempt to shape what Argentina becomes.

### Narrative objective

Present different international readings of Argentina without forcing a permanent alignment. Every approach must feel plausible and incomplete.

### Gameplay objective

Let the player prepare an agenda while preserving alternatives. The player should learn that diplomatic freedom requires work: relationships, resilience, capacity and regional legitimacy.

### What the player is concretely doing

- compares which external relationships fit Argentina's current capacity and political posture;
- chooses whether to prioritise reassurance, autonomy, multipolar dialogue, development partnership or regional leadership;
- prepares a limited offer that Argentina can actually honour;
- decides which strategic sector can be used to open a relationship without surrendering control;
- improves security and intelligence awareness before entering a more exposed diplomatic phase;
- maintains enough flexibility that one foreign answer cannot trap the entire campaign.

### What major powers are doing

- the United States clarifies its initial strategic posture;
- Russia probes whether Argentina seeks partnership or leverage without commitment;
- China tests commercial predictability and long-term stability;
- Europe distinguishes between legal caution, Atlantic pressure and trade pragmatism;
- regional actors ask whether Argentina will share benefits or demand obedience.

### Player emotion

Desirability mixed with danger. The player should feel wanted, watched and tested. This is the first time external interest becomes a source of agency rather than only pressure.

### New opportunities

- competing international openings;
- stronger bargaining leverage when multiple actors show interest;
- first chance to define a practical Argentine agenda;
- possibility of proving that autonomy is an active strategy rather than isolation.

### New responsibilities

- avoid accepting offers that contradict Argentina's stated identity;
- avoid promising access, support or alignment that domestic capacity cannot sustain;
- maintain Latin American legitimacy while engaging distant powers;
- preserve room for future adjustment.

### Perception change

**World:** Argentina is not merely being assessed; it is becoming a potential partner, counterweight, market or leader.

**Inside Argentina:** political actors begin debating what kind of global power Argentina should be and what costs are acceptable.

**New visible objective for the player:** prove one international agenda through a limited, credible act rather than a grand declaration.

### Rhythm

The phase begins with a major diplomatic reveal, then gives the player a medium-length play window. The next moment must occur only after the player has prepared enough capacity to make a meaningful choice.

### Existing Millennium Dawn foundations

- bilateral relationship and recognition patterns support different foreign readings without forcing a binary friendship model;
- international organisation patterns show how membership and alignment can be staged rather than instantly granted;
- market and investment systems support competing partnership offers;
- the Buenos Aires conference precedent supports a limited, visible diplomatic agenda with a clear sequence and consequence.

## 6. Phase IV: The demonstration

### Purpose

The player must prove that Argentina's international language corresponds to a real capacity and a real political discipline.

### Narrative objective

Move Argentina from statement to conduct. A country becomes credible when it can do something difficult, visible and limited without abandoning its principles.

### Gameplay objective

Give the player an operational task that integrates the five activities: diplomacy, economic sovereignty, security, regional stewardship and world-order leadership.

### What the player is concretely doing

- selects one commitment that Argentina can deliver without overextension;
- allocates domestic and continental capacity to support it;
- protects the action from security, market or intelligence disruption;
- coordinates regional expectations and external scrutiny;
- accepts that success will create a lasting obligation or a new audience.

The act should favour a limited commitment over a theatrical one. The player is demonstrating that Argentina can be responsible, not proving that it can dominate every issue at once.

### What major powers are doing

- supportive actors test whether Argentine promises survive pressure;
- cautious actors look for evidence of inconsistency;
- rivals look for overextension or regional resentment;
- markets and partners watch whether Argentina can sustain a commitment without destabilising itself.

### Player emotion

Agency under pressure. This is the first phase in which the player feels the new power fantasy actively: Argentina can shape a situation, but cannot control all of its consequences.

### New opportunities

- earn the first durable increase in Strategic Credibility;
- make one future orientation more plausible without closing all others;
- demonstrate that regional leadership and world diplomacy can reinforce one another;
- create a record of conduct that changes how the next global conversation begins.

### New responsibilities

- deliver what was promised;
- avoid converting a limited undertaking into uncontrolled escalation;
- protect domestic support for an outward-facing policy;
- leave Argentina stronger in resilience, not merely more visible.

### Perception change

**World:** Argentina has shown that it can make an external commitment and carry it through.

**Inside Argentina:** foreign policy stops feeling abstract. Citizens see that global stature requires allocation, risk and discipline.

**New visible objective for the player:** decide which international future best matches the Argentina that has now proven itself.

### Rhythm

The demonstration needs a full operational period. It must not resolve in a single popup or conclude the moment it is announced. The player should feel preparation, execution and consequence.

### Existing Millennium Dawn foundations

- market, investment and trade systems can give material weight to a commitment;
- intelligence and security systems can create protection and exposure;
- relationships can record whether external actors consider the commitment credible;
- the local conference pattern demonstrates that a visible political table can create a sequence of preparation and consequence.

## 7. Phase V: The orientation threshold

### Purpose

Close the opening international crisis without locking Argentina into a permanent camp too early.

### Narrative objective

Argentina understands the world more clearly because the world has now experienced Argentina's conduct, not just its expansion.

### Gameplay objective

Present the player with a readable set of viable geopolitical orientations shaped by previous play. The player should not be rewarded for choosing a label; the player should recognise that prior choices have made some futures easier, harder, richer or more costly.

### What the player is concretely doing

- reviews Argentina's diplomatic position, market confidence, regional legitimacy and security exposure;
- identifies which relationships are genuine and which are merely opportunistic;
- decides whether to deepen one opening, maintain balancing room, lead a wider coalition or seek accommodation;
- prepares the domestic and regional conditions needed for the next act.

### What major powers are doing

- the United States decides whether Argentina is a contained concern, a cautious counterpart or a possible interlocutor;
- Russia and China decide whether to deepen interest or wait for further proof;
- Europe decides whether Argentina is a legal problem, a commercial opportunity or a diplomatic bridge;
- Latin America decides whether Argentine leadership is credible enough to support or dangerous enough to resist.

### Player emotion

Direction. The player should feel that the campaign has opened, not closed. The first crisis is over, but it has made Argentina capable of choosing a future from strength rather than fantasy.

### New opportunities

- choose a primary geopolitical orientation;
- convert the first foreign responses into deeper relationships;
- enter the next act with a coherent agenda;
- use accumulated Strategic Credibility to convene, negotiate or resist pressure.

### New responsibilities

- accept the costs of a more visible international identity;
- prevent a chosen orientation from erasing regional obligations;
- maintain domestic support for a more complex foreign policy;
- continue building the material capacity that makes global ambition sustainable.

### Perception change

**World:** Argentina is no longer only a problem to be managed. It is a power whose chosen direction will affect the larger order.

**Inside Argentina:** the national debate shifts from whether Argentina can rise to how it should use the rise.

**New visible objective for the player:** build a durable Argentine role in the international order.

### Rhythm

The phase ends with a calm synthesis, not a sudden escalation. The player needs time after the orientation threshold to begin the next act's practical work.

### Existing Millennium Dawn foundations

- organisation and alignment systems provide a language for future staged commitment;
- market and investment systems provide the material consequence of a chosen orientation;
- relationship and intelligence systems provide the ongoing cost of visibility;
- regional and conference patterns provide the means to keep Argentine leadership active rather than symbolic.

## 8. The Act I play calendar

The act uses the existing opening crisis screenplay as its narrative spine, but expands it with playable intervals.

| Beat | Form | What happens | Required player time afterward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mexican integration | Threshold | Argentina becomes continental in fact | Brief pause to orient and review capacity |
| World recognition | Narrative moment | The international system notices the change | Short period for observation and stabilisation |
| Global diplomatic debate | Narrative moment | Major powers define competing interpretations | Medium period to consolidate and choose an agenda |
| First external answer | Narrative moment | The first major power turns interpretation into posture | Medium period to prepare a credible response or demonstration |
| Argentine proof | Active gameplay period | The player delivers a limited external commitment | Full operational window for execution and consequence |
| Wider world assessment | Narrative moment | Other actors evaluate conduct, not merely expansion | Medium period for repositioning |
| Orientation threshold | Synthesis | Argentina sees its viable futures | Full period before the next act begins |

### The alternation rule

For every major narrative moment, the player must receive an answerable gameplay question:

- What must I stabilise?
- What can I offer?
- What must I protect?
- Whose trust must I earn?
- Which dependency can I avoid?

If a narrative moment cannot produce such a question, it belongs later in the story or should be removed.

## 9. Systems that support Act I

Act I should be built from existing Millennium Dawn and Argentina Rise foundations. The design does not require a new geopolitical metagame.

### International recognition and relationships

**Supports:** world acknowledgement, foreign caution, bilateral opening, legitimacy and the difference between being noticed and being trusted.

**Why it fits Act I:** the first act is about perception changing before permanent commitments are made.

### Market access, contracts, investment and debt

**Supports:** the proof window, external confidence, development choices, dependence risk and the practical cost of international promises.

**Why it fits Act I:** world politics becomes real when Argentina's capacity to trade, finance and deliver is tested.

### Intelligence and security capacity

**Supports:** strategic awareness, counter-pressure, protection of vulnerable commitments and the feeling that external interest includes scrutiny.

**Why it fits Act I:** Argentina needs to become harder to surprise without treating every foreign contact as a threat.

### International organisation patterns

**Supports:** future alignment, institutional dialogue and the concept that membership brings both identity and obligation.

**Why it fits Act I:** Act I should introduce the language of alignment without granting membership or permanent bloc status.

### The Buenos Aires conference structure

**Supports:** visible agenda progression, player-led diplomatic pacing, a sequence of tables and the feeling that diplomacy has costs and consequences.

**Why it fits Act I:** it is the best local precedent for turning a political meeting into gameplay. The global version must remain rarer, broader and more consequential than the continental original.

### Existing Argentine economic systems

**Supports:** energy, resource development, trade capacity, industry and Treasury pressure as material foundations of international credibility.

**Why it fits Act I:** the player must be able to improve the country between diplomatic moments rather than wait for another conversation.

## 10. Act I completion criteria

Act I is complete when all of the following are true in design terms:

1. The player understands that Mexico changed Argentina's international position.
2. The player has had a substantial operational period after recognition.
3. The player has made at least one limited commitment that tests Strategic Credibility.
4. The player has seen that diplomacy, markets, regional stewardship and security are connected.
5. At least several geopolitical orientations remain plausible, but none is free of cost.
6. Latin American legitimacy still matters as much as distant great-power attention.
7. The player can articulate what Argentina must build next to pursue its preferred direction.

## 11. Critical assessment and readiness

### Strengths of the act

- It gives the Mexican integration a meaningful aftermath.
- It preserves player agency by placing long operational windows between major narrative beats.
- It makes construction, economy and security relevant to diplomacy.
- It introduces global politics gradually instead of overwhelming the player with every world actor at once.
- It leads naturally to a geopolitical choice without treating that choice as a cosmetic label.

### Remaining design questions

Before implementation, three questions still require a separate detailed design pass:

1. What exact limited commitment will serve as the Argentine proof in Phase IV?
2. Which two or three external actors are active in a given playthrough, so the cast remains readable?
3. How will the first operational window visibly communicate Strategic Credibility without adding a redundant universal meter?

These are not conceptual flaws. They are content-selection questions that must be resolved before technical work begins.

### Final verdict

Act I is mature as a Game Design act and is ready to move into detailed content design. It is **not yet ready for direct implementation** until the three questions above are answered and the existing opening screenplay is reconciled with its technical blueprint.

The design foundation is sound: the player does not wait for the world to react. The player performs the work that makes the next world reaction meaningful.
